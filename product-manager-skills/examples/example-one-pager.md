# Saved Search Alerts

## Introduction

- **Owner:** Product Manager
- **Status:** Shaping
- **Last updated:** 2026-06-10

## Problem

People repeatedly run the same searches to discover newly listed items. They must remember their filters and manually check for updates, which makes discovery slower and causes relevant listings to be missed.

**Evidence:** Interview participants described repeating the same search several times per week.

**Size of problem:** TBD. Measure repeat searches using identical filters over a 30-day period.

## Proposed Direction

Allow signed-in customers to save a search and receive an optional notification when new matching items appear.

## Goals and Success Criteria

**Primary objective:** Help customers discover relevant new listings with less repeated effort.

## Scope

**In scope**
- Save a named search with its current filters.
- View and remove saved searches.
- Opt into notifications for a saved search.

**Out of scope**
- Editing individual filters after saving.
- Recommendations unrelated to saved searches.

**Later**
- Notification-frequency controls.

## Dependencies and Partner Needs

- Confirm notification delivery options with the messaging team.
- Confirm retention and consent requirements with legal and privacy reviewers.

## Risks and Open Questions

- Confirm how frequently matching listings should trigger notifications.
- Determine whether a maximum number of saved searches is needed.
